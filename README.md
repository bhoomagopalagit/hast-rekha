# Hast Rekha Palm Reader

A palm reading and Vedic astrology web app that runs in any phone or computer browser.

**Live site:** https://bhoomagopalagit.github.io/hast-rekha/

## Download the Android app

**[Download HastRekha.apk](https://github.com/bhoomagopalagit/hast-rekha/releases/latest/download/HastRekha.apk)** (Android 10 or newer)

1. Open the link above on your Android phone and download the file.
2. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app.
3. Open **Hast Rekha** from your home screen.

The app works offline, opens your gallery or camera for the palm photo, and saves your PDF reading and share card to Downloads. A new APK is built automatically every time the app changes; install it over the old one to update.

## What it does

- **Auto-read (one tap):** the app finds your hand in the photo, lines it up, and reads your hand shape and your heart, head, life and fate lines on your phone, then shows your reading.
- **Palm check (most exact):** line up your palm photo, trace your lines and match what you see to simple pictures, so the reading follows your real hand.
- **Detailed palm reading:** heart, head, life and fate lines, minor lines, mounts, fingers, fingerprints (shankh and chakra), special signs, both-hands comparison and a full "palm in depth" write-up.
- **Vedic birth chart:** Moon rashi, nakshatra, Lagna, North and South Indian charts, navamsa, Vimshottari dasha, Mangal dosha and Sade Sati, calculated from real planet positions (Lahiri ayanamsa).
- **Today:** panchang, Rahu kalam, good days for the next 30 days (Tara Bala and Chandra Bala), a 12-month forecast and biorhythm.
- **Match:** rashi match, Ashtakoota Guna Milan and Palm Match for couples.
- **Share and save:** download the reading as a PDF or a share card image.

Your palm photo stays on your device and is never uploaded or saved.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | The whole app |
| `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` | Lets the app be installed on a phone and work offline |
| `icon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` | App icons |
| `android/` | The Android app: a full-screen wrapper around `index.html` |
| `.github/workflows/android.yml` | Builds the APK on GitHub and publishes it as a release |

## Install from the website instead

Open the live site in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), then choose **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**.
