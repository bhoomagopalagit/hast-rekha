How to add your palm photo

Tap Choose from gallery above. If your gallery doesn't open (some apps block it), copy and paste the photo instead:

  1. Take a clear photo of your open palm in good light.
  2. Open your Photos / Gallery app and open that photo.
  3. Tap Copy. It may be under Share or the ⋮ menu.
  4. Come back to the Palm Reader.
  5. Long-press the dashed box below and tap Paste. No Paste option? Tap the box and insert the photo from your keyboard's clipboard.
  6. Your photo appears on the palm. Tap Read my palm.

In Chrome or Safari, Choose from gallery always opens your photos directly.
